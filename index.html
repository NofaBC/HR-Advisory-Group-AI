Assistant Knowledge Boundary

Omnisource is selected. The Assistant may only retrieve from documents assigned to this client and marked as enabled for Assistant use.

  • No cross-client document retrieval.
  • Drafts identify the client workspace being used.
  • Human review remains required for sensitive HR decisions.

Client Onboarding Flow

1. Create ClientName, contacts, locations, engagement type.
2. Upload DocumentsPolicies, handbook, benefits, payroll, forms, contracts, vendor files.
3. Approve KnowledgeChoose which documents the Assistant may use.
4. Configure WorkstreamsSet priorities, owners, dates, meetings, deliverables.
5. Use AssistantPrepare briefs, comparisons, drafts, follow-ups, decision materials.
6. Maintain WorkspaceReplace documents, update statuses, preserve client separation.
