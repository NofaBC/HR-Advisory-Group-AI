HR Advisory Group AI™

HR Advisory Group AI™ is an integrated AI-assisted HR advisory platform prototype designed to support consistent guidance, stronger documentation, structured workflows, and human-controlled decision-making across four major HR disciplines.

AI assists. HR professionals decide.

Overview

The platform brings four HR advisory functions into one unified workspace:

Recruitment & Retention

Employee Relations & Performance

Compensation & Benefits

Organizational Strategy

The prototype is designed to demonstrate how AI can support HR professionals with intake, clarification, classification, drafting, policy review, risk awareness, scenario analysis, escalation, and follow-up tracking while keeping final decisions with authorized human reviewers.

Core Modules

1. Recruitment & Retention

Supports talent-related advisory workflows, including:

Requisition intake

Hiring brief development

Candidate evaluation support

Interview guide preparation

Retention-risk review

Workforce planning

Cross-module routing to compensation or organizational strategy

2. Employee Relations & Performance

Supports structured HR case management, including:

Case intake

Issue classification

Clarifying questions

Confirmed facts vs. unverified statements

Missing-information identification

Investigation preparation

Risk and escalation flags

Draft advisory work products

Human review and approval

Human-controlled recommendations

4. Organizational Strategy

Organizational structure analysis

Headcount review

Role-overlap identification

Restructuring scenarios

Shared-services modeling

Workforce cost analysis

Executive strategy briefs

Scenario comparison

Shared Policy Library

The platform includes a shared Policy Library concept that can support all four modules.

A production version could allow authorized users to:

Upload approved company policies

Organize policies by HR function

Track versions

Retrieve relevant policy sections

Use policy-aware AI assistance

Maintain access controls

Preserve audit history

The prototype currently uses fictional policy content for demonstration purposes.

Human-in-the-Loop Governance

HR Advisory Group AI™ is designed around a human-controlled decision model.

The AI may assist with:

Organizing facts

Asking clarifying questions

Summarizing cases

Drafting communications

Identifying policy areas

Flagging risk

Generating scenarios

Recommending review paths

The AI does not make final:

Employment decisions

Legal conclusions

Medical determinations

Disciplinary decisions

Termination decisions

Accommodation decisions

Compensation decisions

Benefits decisions

Organizational restructuring decisions

Final decisions remain with authorized HR, legal, compliance, leadership, or other designated reviewers.

Product architecture

AI-assistance points

Human approval points

Cross-module integration

Funder and stakeholder vision
